# BlueHarbor Bank Rewards brand guidelines

## Regulatory language
BlueHarbor is a regulated bank. Every promotional email must include the rewards program disclaimer in the footer, and must not imply that travel rewards are a deposit product or interest.

## Voice
Calm, trustworthy and plain-spoken. Prefer "rewards" over "points". Avoid urgency language such as "act now" or countdown timers.

## Approval
BlueHarbor compliance must approve any email that mentions card benefits, at least ten business days before sending.
