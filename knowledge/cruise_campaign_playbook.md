# Cruise Campaign Playbook

## Who responds
Members who have cruised before convert at roughly three times the rate of first-time cruisers. Recent engagement matters more than tenure: a member who clicked a cruise email in the last six months is the strongest signal we have. The cruise_affinity score in member_360 combines these signals.

## Seasonality
Caribbean sailings sell best when promoted between October and January for winter departures. Alaska is booked early: promote from January through April for May to September sailings. Mediterranean demand peaks with spring promotions for summer departures.

## Offer guidance
Lead with the destination and onboard experience, then the reward value. Higher value segments respond to cabin upgrades more than to discounts. Avoid stacking more than one offer in a single email.
