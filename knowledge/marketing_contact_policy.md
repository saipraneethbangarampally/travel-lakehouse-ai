# Marketing Contact Policy

## Consent requirements
Members resident in the EU, the UK or Canada may only receive promotional email when an explicit opt-in is on record. Members in the United States and Mexico may receive promotional email unless they have opted out. An opt-out always overrides any earlier opt-in, regardless of which system captured it.

Opt-outs must be honoured before the next send. The platform processes consent events as a stream so a preference-center opt-out is reflected within minutes, and audience exports re-check consent at activation time.

## Frequency caps
A member may receive at most two promotional emails per rolling seven days across all campaigns for the same partner brand. Transactional email (booking confirmations, itinerary changes) does not count toward the cap and is never suppressed by marketing consent.

## Exclusions
Do not send a promotion for a product the member has already booked for the same travel window. Members with a confirmed trip departing in the next 30 days should be excluded from acquisition-style cruise and hotel offers.

## Partner isolation
Audiences are always built within a single partner brand. Member data from one partner must never be used to target or enrich members of another partner.
