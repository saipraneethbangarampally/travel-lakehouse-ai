# Summit Airlines Miles brand guidelines

## Voice
Confident, warm and concise. Speak to members as frequent travellers. Use "miles" rather than "points" in every message.

## Offers
Always show the price in miles first and the cash co-pay second. Never use the word "free" for award travel because taxes and fees apply. Diamond members are addressed as "Diamond Members" with capital letters.

## Approval
All cruise and hotel campaigns for SAM require sign-off from the SAM partner manager five business days before the send date.
